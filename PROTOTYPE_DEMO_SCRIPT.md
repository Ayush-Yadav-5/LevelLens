## Part 1: The Classroom Heatmap & Student Diagnostic Drawer
* **Interface Tab:** `Class Map`
* **Duration:** 0:00 – 0:45

### 🎬 On-Screen Walkthrough Guide
1. Start on the default **Class Map** view. Hover or point cursor over the 6×5 physical desk arrangement.
2. Toggle the metric pill from **Oral Reading** to **Foundational Numeracy** and back to show live color transitions (Red = Pre-Word, Orange = Word, Yellow = Sentence, Green = Grade Level).
3. Click on **Desk #01 (Aisha Patel - Grade 3)**.
4. The right-hand **Diagnostic Drawer** slides out. Scroll slightly through Aisha's historical micro-skill breakdown (e.g., letter-sound confusion on `/bh/` vs `/dh/`) and recent WPM trajectory.
5. Click the green button: **"Start Stealth Check"**.

### 🎙️ Spoken Voiceover Script
> "Here on the Class Map, a primary school teacher with 30 multi-grade children across Grades 2, 3, and 4 sees their entire room mapped physically desk-by-desk. Instead of deciphering 30-page register spreadsheets, the teacher instantly spots foundational learning gaps through this ambient heatmap.
> 
> Red desks mark students stuck at the pre-word level, while green indicates grade-level mastery. Toggling between Reading and Numeracy updates the room in real time.
> 
> When I tap on Aisha Patel at Desk 1, her Diagnostic Drawer immediately surfaces her exact micro-skill blockers—not just a vague test score, but diagnostic specifics: she struggles with phonemic blends like /bh/ and /dh/, while her numeracy regrouping is on track. Let’s launch her weekly 60-second diagnostic by clicking 'Start Stealth Check'."

## Part 2: The Stealth Check (Voice AI Oral Reading & Rapid Math)
* **Interface Tab:** `Stealth Check`
* **Duration:** 0:45 – 1:30

### 🎬 On-Screen Walkthrough Guide
1. The **Weekly Stealth Check** screen opens loaded with Aisha Patel and her reading quest passage: *"A tiny cat sat on a mat..."*
2. Click **"Simulate Child Audio"** (or use the microphone if in a quiet room).
3. Watch the real-time speech token alignment: words highlight green as decoded, with flagged hesitations or mispronunciations highlighted in red.
4. Point out the live computed metrics: **WPM (32 WPM)**, **Accuracy (92%)**, and **Latency**.
5. *(Optional Quick Interaction)* Tap one of the words to demonstrate the **1-tap teacher manual override** for noisy classroom environments.
6. Scroll down to the **Rapid Mental Math Challenge**: click the answers for the 3 quick arithmetic prompts (`8 + 5 = 13`, etc.) showing millisecond latency tracking.
7. Click the gradient button: **"Complete & Submit Assessment"**.
8. A celebratory graduation alert pops up: *“🎉 Aisha Patel graduated from Sound Sleuths ➔ Word Builders!”*
9. Click **"View Updated Skill Pods"**.

### 🎙️ Spoken Voiceover Script
> "This is the Stealth Oral Reading Quest. Children in rural government schools often freeze up during high-stakes exams. Here, oral assessment feels like a game.
> 
> As Aisha reads aloud, our lightweight acoustic engine aligns tokens in real time—highlighting accurate phonetic decodes in green and pauses or phoneme substitutions in red. For noisy, low-connectivity classrooms, teachers also have a 1-tap manual override to instantly correct any token.
> 
> Right below, three rapid mental-math flashcards capture latency down to the millisecond, isolating whether a child has conceptual fact fluency or is still finger-counting.
> 
> When I submit this check, watch what happens: Aisha reaches 32 Words Per Minute, breaking through her benchmark threshold. The system immediately graduates her out of the remedial 'Sound Sleuths' pod directly into 'Word Builders'."

## Part 3: Dynamic Cohorting & Micro-Activity Playbooks
* **Interface Tab:** `Skill Pods`
* **Duration:** 1:30 – 2:10

### 🎬 On-Screen Walkthrough Guide
1. Arrive at the **Skill Pods Engine** page.
2. Point out the graduation banner at the top showing Aisha’s movement.
3. Show the 3 fluid Reading Pods: **Sound Sleuths (Pre-Word)**, **Word Builders (Word Level)**, and **Story Explorers (Grade Level)**. Note how Aisha Patel is now listed inside *Word Builders*.
4. Highlight that these cohorts are **cross-grade** (Grades 2, 3, and 4 learning side-by-side without stigma).
5. Click **"Auto-Regroup Classroom"** to showcase how all 30 students re-cluster into balanced peer groups based on recent mastery data.
6. Expand one of the **10-Minute Low-Resource Micro-Activity Cards** (e.g., *"Chalk Phoneme Hopscotch"* or *"Bottle Cap Regrouping"*).

### 🎙️ Spoken Voiceover Script
> "This brings us to the Skill Pods Engine. In a traditional multi-grade classroom, teaching the prescribed Grade 3 textbook leaves lagging students behind forever.
> 
> LevelLens replaces static age-grades with fluid, competency-based pods. Notice that Aisha has already moved into 'Word Builders' alongside Grade 2 and Grade 4 peers at her exact skill level—completely removing age stigma.
> 
> Clicking 'Auto-Regroup' rebalances the whole room dynamically in milliseconds.
> 
> Most importantly, teachers don't need expensive gadgets or smartboards. Each pod comes with a 10-minute micro-activity designed for zero-resource environments—using physical chalk on the veranda floor, stones, bottle caps, and slate boards."

## Part 4: The Administrative Win & Parent Engagement Loop
* **Interface Tab:** `FLN Compliance`
* **Duration:** 2:10 – 2:45

### 🎬 On-Screen Walkthrough Guide
1. Click the **FLN Compliance** tab in the top navigation bar.
2. Point out the **stacked progress bars** showing functional readers (Sentence + Grade Level) and numeracy mastery across the entire school cohort.
3. Scroll to the **National Benchmark Mapping** section, highlighting standard NIPUN Bharat indicators (`L2.1`, `L2.4`, `L3.1`, `M2.1`, `M3.1`).
4. Scroll down to the **Parent Loop** section:
   - Select **Aisha Patel** from the child dropdown.
   - Click **"Simulate Parent Audio"** to play the synthesized 60-second Hindi/English audio voice dispatch.
   - Point out **"Today's 2-Minute Home Game"** (*"Find the First Sound in the Kitchen"*).
5. Scroll to the top and click the glowing green button: **"Generate Official FLN Report"**.
6. The **Official NIPUN Bharat FLN Audit Document** modal appears with official letterhead, UDISE+ school code, competency matrix, CRC stamp area, and **Export CSV / Print PDF** actions.

### 🎙️ Spoken Voiceover Script
> "Finally, we solve the biggest pain point for government school educators: administrative paperwork.
> 
> Every classroom micro-skill is mapped automatically to official Ministry of Education NIPUN Bharat FLN competency codes like L2.1 and M3.1. Zero weekend data entry.
> 
> For community outreach, our Parent Loop generates automated 60-second IVR voice notes and 2-minute daily verbal games that require zero literacy from parents—like finding kitchen items that start with the /k/ sound.
> 
> With one click on 'Generate Official FLN Report', the teacher gets an audit-ready government report complete with UDISE+ verification, competency percentages, and instant CSV export for state portal uploads. What previously took days of paperwork is now an automatic byproduct of daily teaching."

## Presenter Pro-Tips for Hackathon Delivery
1. **Pacing:** Keep a brisk, energetic delivery (~140-150 words per minute).
2. **Audio Fallback:** In a crowded or loud judging hall, always use the **"Simulate Child Audio"** and **"Simulate Parent Audio"** buttons—they guarantee 100% reliable acoustics without microphone ambient noise interference.
3. **Contrast:** Emphasize the shift from *anxiety-inducing written exams* to *stealth voice play*, and *tedious weekend paperwork* to *1-click UDISE+ compliance*.
